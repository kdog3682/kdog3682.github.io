  en={`Seventeen years since you rode the crane to the west; we still meet in dreams, in tears.
Forty years of mutual respect and love; an affection deep as the sea, cherished forever.

Thankful for my parents' care in raising us, Qiongying was devoted to her work.
She bred new, fine varieties of sesame, with brilliant results that people praised.

Varieties No. 7 and No. 5 were spread together; the joy of harvest filled the farm families.
The Ministry of Agriculture honored her work; deeply encouraged, she kept striving.

My virtuous wife was of noble character; selfless and dutiful, a model to all.
She cared for kin and friends and loved to give; though she has passed, everyone remembers her.

Devoted to her parents and loving to her husband, fifty springs and autumns with one heart.
Time rushes by and does not return; but may we renew our bond in the next life!`}

<Poem
  client:load
  title="聪慧的先母——黄本花老太君"
  author="李唐勛"
  zh={`年方十九嫁父亲，体贴入微待公婆。
关爱弟妹如手足，家庭和睦邻里夸。

嫁夫无房不计较，粗茶淡饭不嫌贫。
助夫创业不畏难，同艰共苦奔前程。

致富以后不忘本，怜贫帮困视己任。
对待雇工如家人，一视同仁共忧欢。

勤劳聪慧过常人，持家有方名远扬。
自学医药开处方，免费义诊惠乡亲。

吾母禀性极淳厚，慈悲为怀待他人。
呕心沥血育子孙，家道兴旺有母功。`}
  en={`At nineteen she married my father; she attended her parents-in-law with every tender care.
She loved his younger siblings like her own; the household was harmonious, praised by neighbors.

She did not mind that he had no house; nor coarse tea and plain rice, nor poverty.
She helped him build a business, unafraid of hardship; sharing every trial, they pressed toward the future.

Once prosperous, she never forgot her roots; to pity the poor and aid the needy was her duty.
She treated hired workers like family; she was fair to all, sharing sorrow and joy.

Hardworking and wise beyond the ordinary, she ran her home so well that her name spread far.
Self-taught in medicine, she wrote prescriptions; she treated villagers free of charge.

My mother was deeply kind and genuine by nature; she held compassion in her heart for everyone.
She poured her heart and blood into raising her descendants; the family's prosperity is her achievement.`}
/>

<Poem
  client:load
  title="伟大的先父——双璧公"
  author="李唐勛"
  zh={`出身寒门不自卑，务农辛苦笑面对。
人品举止人人夸，农会主席众推他。

红色风暴卷家乡，父闹革命当先锋。
革命受挫未成功，英烈鲜血洒神州。

伪府通缉抓捕父，背井离乡窜四方。
受尽苦难和折磨，潜逃深山当矿工。

借资创业挖煤窑，经营有方获利丰。
富后不忘穷苦人，行善公益分国忧。

捐出巨资办小学，福泽桑梓育后裔。
合资办厂兴业多，发展经济利地方。

孝敬父母爱手足，双亲后事一人包。
教育儿女抓根本，德才兼备不放松。

缅怀先父当年情，白手创业极艰辛。
儿孙能有今日福，莫忘父母养育恩。`}
  en={`Born to a poor family, he was never ashamed; he farmed through hardship and faced it smiling.
His character and conduct were praised by all; the peasant association chose him as chairman.

The red storm swept through our home region; my father joined the revolution as a vanguard.
The revolution suffered defeat and did not succeed; the blood of heroes was shed across the land.

The puppet government issued a warrant for his arrest; he left home and fled in all directions.
He endured suffering and torment; he escaped deep into the mountains and became a miner.

He borrowed capital to dig a coal kiln; he managed it well and profited greatly.
Rich, he did not forget the poor; he did good and public works, sharing the country's burdens.

He donated a large sum to found a primary school; his kindness to his hometown blessed later generations.
He co-founded many factories and enterprises; he developed the economy for the benefit of the region.

He honored his parents and loved his siblings; he alone took care of both parents' final affairs.
He raised his children on fundamentals; he never relaxed in demanding both virtue and talent.

Remembering my father's early days: starting from nothing, the hardship was extreme.
That his children and grandchildren enjoy such fortune today, let them never forget their parents' care.`}
